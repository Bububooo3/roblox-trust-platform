### Raw Overview
- There’s no real-money payment system made specifically for Roblox game development.

- People many times don’t have the same apps for payment methods
- People often get scammed out of their work and scammers get away free

- If possible, make it so users can easily create contracts (reach goal)

- There’s no universal way of tracking the reputation (trustworthiness) of different developers

- There are local trackers that vary from discord server to discord server
- Developers can get rated out of 5 stars by people who completed transactions with them
- Developers can get reported for scamming either for not paying or not doing work and reports get reviewed and voted on by users who opt in for the “Community Scam Catcher” program (decisions can be overridden by higher-ups)

- On Roblox Talent hub there is no automatic work history, so you have to remember everything you did

- Instead developers could automatically track transactions (all of which must have descriptions) and choose which ones they want to make public

- Successfully reported users get a mark on that specific transaction that says “reported” and it’s forced to be public

- Victims just get a “scammed” instead of “cancelled” or “successful” but it’s not forced to be public

- Developers can cancel but both sides of the transaction have to agree. Otherwise a scam investigation is opened that will either result in a success or a report or a cancel depending on whether the two parties reconcile

- Instead of subtracting an amount like Roblox does, add a transaction fee that is less than that of Roblox but still profitable

- Other reach goals

- Make a way to group transactions and payouts
